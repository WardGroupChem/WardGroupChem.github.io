Jonathan Ward Chemistry Group Research Site

Fluorescent materials, robust photocatalysts, molecular electronics and antibacterial CO-releasing materials

Interdisciplinary Research

Organic synthesis, molecular design, physical measurements

Carbon monoxide-releasing materials

We are designing materials to kill bacteria/prevent biofilm formation

Fluorescent Materials

We are making fluorescent materials that self-assemble on surfaces for various optical/optoelectronic applications. Demonstration procedures for oscillating luminol clock available on the RSC website. Highly recommended! link to RSC page

Molecular Electronics

We have an interest in preparing pi-conjugated molecules and tuning the electrical and thermal conductance

Robust photocatalysts

Development of new pi-conjugated photocatalysts with strong-bonding throughout the system

Royal Society Research Grant Start (1st Dec 23)

I'm very excited to announce the start of my Royal Society Research Grant where I will be using my expertise in optoelectronics to develop an array of bespoke pi-conjugated molecules for use in photocatalysis. The grant has been used to purchase Asynt and Uniqsis batch reactors and a flow reactor for photocatalyst development.
