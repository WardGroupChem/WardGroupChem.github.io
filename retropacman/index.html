Disconnect// the full maze — 8 questions, 2 rooms

Run the maze. Solve all 8 disconnections.

Clear all 4 checkpoints in a room to unlock its exit door and move on to the next. Ghosts join as you go — two to start, a third as you enter room two, and a fourth once you're three questions into it.

  • Arrow keys / WASD to move (or the on-screen pad)
  • 3 lives — ghost contact or a wrong answer costs one
  • In-game score: 1 point per dot, 5 per correct answer (just for fun — not sent to Moodle)

The dashed red bond is the one to disconnect.
