Synthesize// space invaders — alkenes & alkynes

Defend the lab.

Clear the invaders while reagent capsules drift down every so often. Catch one — shoot it, or just let it arrive — and answer the synthesis question to upgrade your bullets.

  • Arrow keys / A-D to move, Space to fire (or the on-screen buttons)
  • Bullets start as alkane (—), upgrade to alkene (=) then alkyne (≡) on correct answers — alkyne shots pierce through a whole column
  • A wrong answer costs a life and drops your bullet back a tier
  • Crystal bunkers give you cover — they take damage from either side and stay damaged
  • Invaders occasionally fire back, and reaching the bottom also costs a life
  • Every ~10s a shield pickup (hex with a shield icon) also drifts down — touch it or shoot it to block the next alien shot
  • 8 questions total — score 70% or higher to pass
